[Original Workshop page](https://reforger.armaplatform.com/workshop/66D61CE15CC684A6)

Standalone adaptation and integration by **BlackFox**, including settings, personal garage rules, world claim limits, UID consistency, parking-spot allocation and native persistence integration.

The retained legacy cargo helpers are original BlackFox code. Current garage parking and retrieval do not call those helpers.
